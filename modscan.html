modscan

Modpacks from a random link can carry account stealers. modscan looks inside every mod before you launch the game, and tells you, file by file, what looks dangerous.

Download How it works

modscan

Check a modpack before you play it.

Drop in a modpack and get a verdict for every file. The mods Modrinth already knows are set aside by their exact hash; everything else is read down to the bytecode, without ever running it.

LOW MEDIUM HIGH Cannot be judged What every tag means
Any pack

Whatever you downloaded

An .mrpack, a CurseForge or instance zip, a mods folder or a single jar. Or pick an instance straight from Prism, CurseForge, the Modrinth App or the official launcher.

Modrinth check

Trusted mods set aside

Every file is matched against Modrinth by its exact hash. A mod you got from somewhere else, or one that was tampered with, does not match.

Deep analysis

Down to the bytecode

Known stealer and dropper patterns, strings hidden or rebuilt at runtime, code that downloads and runs other programs, bundled libraries that were modified.

Honest verdicts

"Cannot be judged" when it can't

When the code a file runs is not in the file, because it is fetched or decrypted later, modscan says so instead of calling it clean.

Known threats

Shared warnings

Files that the Studio Deriva team has already marked as malicious come back as a warning in your result.

Reports

Export and share

Save the result as text or JSON, to keep it or to send it to whoever gave you the pack.

Your files stay on your computer

Modpacks and mods are never uploaded. Modrinth only receives the hashes of your files, and modscan downloads from Modrinth the mods a pack lists but does not contain.

After each scan the app saves the hash, name and result of the analysed mods in the modscan database, to improve the scanner and to warn you about files already known to be malicious.

The scores are heuristics from static analysis, not an antivirus verdict. LOW does not prove that a file is safe.
