Pumpkin carving, pixel by pixel

Carves & Crafts adds a carving bench where you cut and paint pumpkins pixel by pixel. Cuts go all the way through the wall, so you see the flesh and the hollow inside; paint takes any colour; a torch or a candle turns the finished pumpkin into a lantern that glows in the colour of its flame.

Free and open source, like everything we make: MIT, source included, no premium version.

Features

  • Nine pumpkin models, each with its own shape and skin: Classic, White, Blue, Cinderella, Mini Yellow, Warty, Butternut, Kabocha and Turban.
  • Real holes. A cut pixel goes through the wall and shows the flesh, the hole's sides and the cavity behind it.
  • Paint in any colour. Full RGB with a hex field, HSV picker, dye swatches, fill, eyedropper, mirror, undo and redo. Three canvas densities: x1, x2 and x4.
  • Lanterns. Torch, soul torch, copper torch, redstone torch or any candle: vanilla light level, glowing inside in its colour.
  • Tools that wear out. The knife loses one durability per cut pixel, the brush one charge per painted pixel. A Color Palette refills the brush.
  • Schematics. Save designs, copy them onto blank pumpkins, and share them as .pumpkin files. Data packs can ship presets.
  • Farming and worldgen. Field Pumpkin, Heirloom Pumpkin and Winter Squash seeds, and wild patches in matching biomes.

Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API and Java 21. Install it on both the client and the server. Optional: Mod Menu, JEI, Jade or WTHIT.
