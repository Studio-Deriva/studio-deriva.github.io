Carves & Crafts

Pumpkin carving, pixel by pixel.

A carving bench where you cut and paint pumpkins pixel by pixel. Cuts go all the way through the wall, and a torch or a candle turns the finished pumpkin into a lantern that glows in the colour of its flame.

Try the bench

Carve this one.

Drag on the pumpkin to cut through the skin, paint it, then light it. In the mod the knife loses one durability per cut pixel and the brush one charge per painted pixel.

Knife: 0 durability used Brush: 0 charges used

Nine models

Every pumpkin its own shape

Classic, White, Blue, Cinderella, Mini Yellow, Warty, Butternut, Kabocha and Turban. Warts, stems and leaves stay; the body is what you carve.

Real holes

Cut through the wall

A cut pixel shows the flesh, the hole's sides and the cavity behind it. Warts sitting over a cut disappear with it.

Any colour

A real paint editor

Full RGB with hex field, HSV picker, dye swatches, fill, eyedropper, mirror, undo and redo, zoom and pan. Canvas at x1, x2 or x4.

Lanterns

Light that matches the flame

Torch, soul torch, copper torch, redstone torch or any candle. Vanilla light level, glowing inside in its colour.

Schematics

Save, copy, share

Keep a library per world, export designs to .pumpkin files, import your friends'. Data packs can ship presets.

Farming

Seeds and wild patches

Field Pumpkin, Heirloom Pumpkin and Winter Squash seeds, each growing a weighted mix of models. Wild patches spawn in matching biomes.

BetterTrades

Trading for Cobblemon, done safely.

Server-side player trading for Cobblemon. Pokémon, items and money in the same offer, a full history in a database, and no client mod required: vanilla clients see a normal container.

Try a trade

The real trade screen, in your browser.

The mod's own GUI textures and slot grid, with Minecraft's and Cobblemon's item textures and Minecraft's font. Offer items, Pokémon and money, confirm, and watch the three second countdown. The other player, Alex, is played by the page.

Click: whole stack · Right click: one · Shift click: half · Hover for details

How a trade stays safe

Nothing moves until everything checks out.

A trading mod moves other people's items, Pokémon and money. A duplication bug ends a server's economy, so every step is built to fail closed.

  1. OfferUp to eight slots per side: items, Pokémon and money together. Opened from Cobblemon's own interaction wheel.
  2. EscrowOffered items leave the inventory at once and are recorded in a local database. Crash mid-trade, and they come back at next login.
  3. FingerprintPokémon never move early. They're fingerprinted on offer and re-checked at commit; if anything changed, the trade is cancelled.
  4. CommitOnly the money difference moves, in one atomic Impactor operation. Trade evolutions and Cobblemon's events still fire.
No client mod

Built with Polymer and sgui

The screen is server-side. Players just need the resource pack, which Polymer can host for you.

History

Every trade, stored

A normalised schema on MariaDB or MySQL, with automatic SQLite fallback and replay when the remote database comes back.

Blacklist

Down to species and form

Block items and Pokémon by species, form and aspects, editable in game with /bt blacklist.

API

Read-only, for other mods

Per-mod keys and two levels: history queries, or queries plus cancellable pre-trade events.

Your words

Every message is a file

English and Italian ship with the mod. Rewrite any line, colour codes included, and reload with /bt db lang.

Admin

See and stop trades

/bt active lists trades in progress, /bt cancel stops one, /bt history shows anyone's past trades.
