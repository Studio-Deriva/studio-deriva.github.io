The one exception is private commissioned work. Code someone asked and paid us to write belongs to them, so it stays theirs to publish or not.

Suggestions

Your ideas actually ship.

Most of what we've shipped began as somebody's message in our suggestions channel. Write yours down, even if it sounds small. An idea with the why filled in gets built far more often than a one-liner.

If your idea ships, your name goes in the credits of the mod and in the release notes. If it turns into something big, we go further, and we tell you before the release, not after.

  • One idea per message. Five ideas in one post get answered as zero.
  • Scroll first. If it's already there, react to it instead of posting it again.
  • What gets built stays free and open source, like everything else we make.
#suggestions
**Idea:** one line, the whole thing
**What it does:** blocks, items, mobs, mechanics
**Why it's worth building:** what's missing without it
**Version / loader:** if it only makes sense on one
**References:** links, screenshots, mods that come close
